What the work can include, and what affects timing and cost

Roofing work starts with understanding the condition of the roof and the work you’re considering. The next steps depend on the inspection findings and the scope. A repair, full replacement, metal roof, or flat roof project will each have different requirements. Timing can also depend on the work involved, the condition of the roof, and weather. Ask the contractor to explain the expected schedule for your specific project rather than relying on a general estimate.

Key facts to discuss:
- Coverage: Confirm whether the recommendation is for a roof repair or replacement, and whether gutters, siding, or skylights are part of the work.
- Timing: Ask when the inspection can take place and what may affect the start and completion of the work.
- Cost factors: The type and extent of work, roof materials, and any additional needs identified during inspection can affect the overall cost. Ask for the scope and cost to be explained in writing.

A few common terms help during these conversations. A roof replacement means removing and replacing the roofing system. Storm damage restoration refers to work addressing damage associated with a storm. A flat roof has a low-slope design, unlike a typical pitched roof.
